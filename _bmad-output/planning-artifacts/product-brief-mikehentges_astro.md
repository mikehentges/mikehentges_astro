# Product Brief: Hentges.AI

## Executive Summary

Hentges.AI LLC needs a professional web presence that matches the caliber of its principal's expertise. Mike Hentges brings 20+ years as CTO of a Fortune 500 systems integrator, 3 years as IT Director at a mid-sized manufacturer, and deep applied AI experience — but the current site is a personal hobby blog about technology, chess, and woodworking. The gap between credibility and web presence is the problem.

The rewrite transforms Hentges.AI into a narrative-led consulting site that communicates a clear worldview: *AI is an accelerator, not a shortcut — and you still need someone who knows how to build the right thing, the right way, for your situation.* The site leads with client empowerment, demonstrates expertise through thought leadership content, and makes it easy for the right prospects to reach out. It's a credibility vehicle first, discoverable through LinkedIn shares and organic search second.

This is a complete redesign — new visual identity, modern tech stack (Astro with Tailwind CSS v4), Cloudflare Pages deployment, and a lean five-route architecture built on confident restraint.

## The Problem

Mike operates a consulting practice focused on helping organizations extract real value from AI — not through hype, but through architecture, judgment, and production-grade execution. His prospects are mid-market VPs trying to move from "playing with ChatGPT" to real business impact, SI firm leaders tired of AI demos that break in production, and non-technical CEOs burned by off-the-shelf tools that don't fit.

These prospects will Google him. They'll click a LinkedIn link. What they find today is a personal blog with a generic tagline ("Technology, Programming, Chess, and Woodworking"), system fonts, and no business positioning. It undermines credibility before a conversation even starts.

In a market where every services firm is now claiming AI expertise, trust is the primary buying signal. Prospects evaluate personal credibility — writing, experience, worldview — before evaluating the firm. A weak web presence is not a cosmetic problem; it's a business problem.

## The Solution

A professional, narrative-led website that positions Hentges.AI as an experienced guide with a clear point of view. The site communicates expertise through worldview and thought leadership rather than service catalogs or industry verticals. The design should be designed to work for both readers and skimmers — the first viewport delivers a clear "who I am, what I do, who I do it for" signal before the deeper narrative unfolds.

**Homepage flow:** Empowerment-led hero ("Help your team create more value, solve problems faster, use AI to its full potential") followed by a point-of-view block, career proof points, recent blog posts, a "how I work" section, and a simple call to action.

**Five lean routes:**
- **/** — Homepage with narrative positioning
- **/blog** — Thought leadership with broad scope (AI, development practices, technology trends)
- **/blog/[slug]** — Individual posts
- **/about** — Professional narrative weaving three career threads (enterprise systems builder, manufacturing operations leader, AI practitioner) into a cohesive story of boundary-crossing expertise
- **/contact** — Direct email with LinkedIn link, no forms or funnels. Includes a soft engagement signal: "I take on a small number of advisory and project engagements — no long-term staff augmentation."

**Blog strategy:** 1-2 posts per month with strong point of view. Content is unified by a brand promise: *you'll leave with a clearer, less fashionable, more useful view of AI than you arrived with.* Topics span AI in practice, development methodology, and technology trends — broad enough to demonstrate domain mastery, anchored by the consulting worldview. Flagship launch content: "What People Are Getting Wrong About AI" — a contrarian piece challenging the cost-reduction narrative, backed by operational credibility.

**Design direction:** Confident, editorial, slightly irreverent — closer to a respected CTO's Substack than a consulting firm's brochure. Professional without being corporate. The aesthetic should signal craft and restraint, not template.

## What Makes This Different

The site's positioning is built on five authentic brand pillars that emerged from deep career archaeology:

1. **The Builder Who Reads Systems** — Sees architecture behind interfaces, not just user-level functionality
2. **Problem Translator** — Dual fluency between business intent and technical reality, producing creative solutions instead of obvious ones
3. **AI Amplifies Architects** — AI made the hard skills (problem decomposition, architecture, judgment) more important, not less
4. **Production, Not Prototypes** — 20+ years knowing what production-ready actually requires
5. **Operator, Not Just Advisor** — Has run the P&L, built and managed teams, sold and delivered to Fortune 500 clients

Most solo AI consultants have weak web presence and vague positioning. This site backs its claims with operational credibility that can't be fabricated.

**AI governance and responsible deployment:** As regulatory pressure increases (EU AI Act, emerging US state laws), organizations need advisors who understand the security structure of applying AI — not just what's possible, but what's responsible. This fits squarely within the "do more than play around" positioning and elevates the practice beyond pure acceleration into risk-aware architecture. Governance content and expertise is woven into the consulting worldview, not offered as a separate service line.

## Who This Serves

The site speaks to **technology leaders responsible for making AI work in their organization** — a single narrative that blends two primary personas rather than trying to address everyone:

**Primary: Mid-market VP / IT Director**
Stuck between AI experimentation and real business value. Has a team they want to empower, not replace. Needs an experienced guide who understands both the technology and the organizational reality. Recognition signal: *"Make your team 100x more valuable."*

**Primary: SI Firm CEO / CTO**
Running a delivery organization where AI-built solutions need to survive production, not just impress in demos. Needs a peer who has operated at this level — someone who has run the P&L, staffed the teams, and answered the 2 AM calls. Recognition signal: *"Production, not prototypes."*

These two audiences share a core need: they want AI to create durable value, not theater. The messaging unifies around empowerment and production-readiness. Non-technical CEOs may also find the site through referral, but the voice and vocabulary are calibrated for leaders who live at the intersection of technology and business outcomes.

## Success Criteria

- **Credibility signal:** Prospects who Google Mike or click a LinkedIn link find a professional site that reinforces (not undermines) his expertise
- **Content discoverability:** Blog posts rank for relevant long-tail AI consulting terms; LinkedIn-shared posts drive site visits. Realistic baseline: organic search traffic growing month-over-month within 6 months of consistent publishing
- **Engagement quality:** Inbound inquiries come from qualified prospects who self-selected based on the site's positioning — even one qualified inquiry per quarter justifies the investment for a lifestyle practice
- **Sustainable cadence:** 1-2 blog posts per month is maintainable alongside consulting work and semi-retirement lifestyle

## Scope

**In for launch:**
- Complete visual redesign with professional typography, color system, and layout
- Astro (current version) + Tailwind CSS v4 with component architecture (Tailwind Plus)
- Cloudflare Pages deployment
- Five-route site: Home, Blog, Blog Post, About, Contact
- Blog content migration: technology and AI posts migrate; hobby content (chess, woodworking) is retired entirely — no redirects needed, clean break to business-focused identity
- 1-2 new blog posts reinforcing brainstorm themes
- SEO fundamentals (meta tags, structured data, sitemap)
- LinkedIn follow CTA on blog posts and contact page as low-friction "stay connected" option

**Out of scope:**
- Productized offerings or service packages (not defined yet)
- Contact form or CRM integration (direct email by design)
- Case studies or portfolio section (case narratives live in blog)
- Advanced analytics — Cloudflare's built-in metrics are sufficient for a lifestyle practice; revisit if diagnostic needs arise
- Multi-author or CMS features
- Newsletter infrastructure (consider post-launch if content cadence proves sustainable)

## Vision

Hentges.AI stays lean. This is a lifestyle consulting practice — a way to do interesting work on Mike's terms, not a growth business. The site serves as a durable credibility platform that:

- Compounds authority through consistent, opinionated content over time
- Builds an organic SEO moat in the AI consulting space
- Supports selective engagement — the right clients find Mike, not the other way around
- Evolves content scope naturally as the practice encounters new domains and challenges

If it works well, the site becomes the kind of place people bookmark and share — not because it's a marketing machine, but because the person behind it consistently says things worth reading.
