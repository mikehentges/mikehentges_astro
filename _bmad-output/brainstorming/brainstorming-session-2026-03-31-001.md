# Brainstorming Session Results

**Facilitator:** Mike
**Date:** 2026-03-31

## Session Overview

**Topic:** Reimagine Hentges.AI from a personal blog into a professional consulting business site for Hentges.AI LLC

**Goals:**
- Craft a compelling professional narrative around three pillars: CTO/systems integration leadership (20+ years, Fortune 500 contact center solutions), IT Director at a mid-sized manufacturer (3 years), and applied AI research/tooling
- Shift from blog-centric to business-centric site architecture
- Evaluate tech stack shift (currently Astro → potentially Nuxt 4/Vue/NuxtUI)
- Position AI expertise as a core differentiator for prospective clients

### Session Setup

Mike is the principal of Hentges.AI LLC, a consulting business focused on software development processes. The existing site is a personal blog built on Astro 4 with Markdown content and Cloudinary-hosted images. The rewrite aims to transform this into a business-forward presence that communicates credibility, expertise, and value to potential consulting clients.

## Technique Selection

**Approach:** AI-Recommended Techniques
**Analysis Context:** Consulting business site rebrand with focus on professional narrative, audience-validated positioning, and concrete architecture

**Recommended Techniques:**

- **Values Archaeology:** Excavate the authentic throughlines across 23+ years of career to define brand pillars and value proposition
- **Role Playing:** Stress-test positioning through stakeholder perspectives (potential clients, referral partners, competitors)
- **Morphological Analysis:** Systematically map site structure, content types, tech stack, and conversion paths into concrete architecture options

**AI Rationale:** This sequence moves from internal clarity (what makes Mike's offering unique) → external validation (what audiences need to see) → concrete decisions (how to build it). Each phase feeds the next, preventing the common trap of jumping straight to site structure before the brand story is clear.

## Technique Execution Results

### Values Archaeology

**Interactive Focus:** Excavating authentic brand pillars from 23+ years of career experience across systems integration, manufacturing IT leadership, and AI research.

**Key Ideas Generated:**

**[Brand Pillar #1]**: The Builder Who Reads Systems
_Concept_: Mike doesn't learn tools as a user — he reverse-engineers them as an architect. He sees SQL tables behind screens, data flow between steps, processing logic that most users experience as a black box. He understands not just what a system does but how it wants to work.
_Novelty_: Most consultants are either technical (they build) or operational (they use). Mike collapses that gap — he can sit with business users AND read the system's intent at the architectural level.

**[Brand Pillar #2]**: Problem Translator
_Concept_: Mike operates at the boundary between business intent and technical reality. He pulls users out of solution-speak ("build me a button") back to problem-speak ("what are you trying to accomplish"), then creatively matches technology capabilities to the real need.
_Novelty_: Dual fluency in what the user actually needs AND what the technology can actually do, including its limitations. That dual fluency produces creative solutions instead of obvious ones.

**[Brand Pillar #3]**: AI Amplifies Architects, Not Replaces Developers
_Concept_: AI solved the language barrier of software development — knowing syntax, frameworks, API patterns. But it made the skills that were always the hard part even MORE important: understanding the real problem, designing the right architecture, breaking complex needs into logical steps. AI also lowers implementation cost enough that niche problems previously too small to justify custom software are now solvable.
_Novelty_: Contrarian to the "AI replaces developers" narrative. The field tilted toward people who know how to build the right thing. Opens new market for smaller-scale solutions.

**[Brand Pillar #4]**: Production, Not Prototypes
_Concept_: The current AI-assisted development landscape is flooded with impressive demos that fall apart in production. Mike's 20+ years of building systems Fortune 500 companies actually ran in production means he knows what production-ready requires: redundancy, security, observability, maintainability.
_Novelty_: Timely market position. The gap between "it works on my laptop" and "it runs reliably at scale" creates massive demand for this expertise. Pro-rigor with AI, not anti-AI.

**[Brand Pillar #5]**: Operator, Not Just Advisor
_Concept_: Mike has run the business — CTO of an SI firm means scoping and selling solutions to enterprise clients, building and managing delivery teams, setting product/capability direction, managing P&L, and ensuring customer satisfaction. This is operational experience at the leadership level, not theoretical.
_Novelty_: Most technical consultants advise. Mike has operated. He knows what it takes to sell, staff, deliver, support, and make profitable. Opens broader client profile beyond "companies building software."

**Unified Brand Thesis:** "I help organizations build the right thing, the right way, faster — using AI as an accelerator, not a shortcut."

**Creative Breakthroughs:**
- The "boundary-crosser" pattern — Mike consistently shows up at the intersection between domains, between business and technical, between architecture and usage
- The niche solutions insight — AI makes previously uneconomical custom solutions viable
- The broader client profile — not just software companies but SI firms adopting AI, manufacturers evaluating build-vs-buy, companies standing up development practices

### Role Playing

**Interactive Focus:** Testing brand pillars and messaging through the eyes of three target client personas visiting the site.

**Key Ideas Generated:**

**[Persona Insight #1]**: The VP's Real Pain — From Toy to Tool
_Concept_: Mid-market VPs are stuck between "playing with ChatGPT" and "AI making the business better." The answer isn't always custom software — sometimes it's empowering existing people to use AI tools in their own jobs. This expands Hentges.AI's scope beyond software development into broader AI-enabled business value.
_Novelty_: Reframes the offering from "software development consultant" to "helping organizations extract real business value from AI" — whether through custom solutions or user empowerment.

**[Persona Insight #2]**: The Anti-Replacement Message
_Concept_: "Don't use AI to get rid of your team — use it to make your team 100x more valuable." Positions Mike as the opposite of the threat narrative. Attracts leaders who value their people and want to invest in them.
_Novelty_: Most AI consultants implicitly sell efficiency through headcount reduction. This positioning attracts long-term relationship clients.

**[Persona Insight #3]**: The 2 AM Test
_Concept_: "Are you tired of AI-built systems that wake you up in the middle of the night?" Instant tribal signal for SI firm operators. Signals production responsibility, not demo responsibility.
_Novelty_: Language only someone who has run an SI firm would use. Separates Mike from AI consultants who've never had to support what they built.

**[Persona Insight #4]**: The Custom-Fit Promise
_Concept_: "Stop being shoehorned into tools that don't fit. AI now makes it possible to build something crafted for your business." Speaks to non-technical CEOs burned by off-the-shelf solutions and failed freelancer projects.
_Novelty_: Connects to the niche solutions insight — AI lowered the cost of custom software enough that previously unjustifiable custom builds are now viable.

**Audience Recognition Signal Map:**

| Audience | Recognition Signal | Pillar Connection |
|----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Mid-market VP/IT Director | "Make your team 100x more valuable" | AI Amplifies Architects |
| SI firm CEO/CTO | "Tired of 2 AM wake-up calls?" | Production, Not Prototypes |
| Non-technical CEO | "Built for your business, not shoehorned" | Problem Translator + Niche Solutions |

**Unifying Theme:** "AI changed what's possible — but you still need someone who knows how to build the right thing, the right way, for YOUR situation."

### Morphological Analysis

**Interactive Focus:** Systematically mapping site structure, content strategy, tech stack, and conversion paths into concrete architecture decisions.

**Key Ideas Generated:**

**[Site Architecture #1]**: Narrative-Led, Thought Leadership First
_Concept_: Lead with worldview and expertise, not service categories or industry verticals. The site says "here's how I think about technology and business problems" and lets visitors self-qualify. No audience lanes — Mike's brand is about being a boundary-crosser who adapts to any domain.
_Novelty_: Avoids looking like a small firm pretending to be a big consultancy with neat service boxes. Feels like what it is — a seasoned expert with a point of view.

**[Site Architecture #2]**: Content Strategy — Blog with Broad Scope
_Concept_: Blog section publishing 1-2 posts/month with strong point of view. Called "Blog" not "Perspectives" — keeps it casual enough to cover AI, Rust, development practices, industry trends. The variety demonstrates the "rapid domain mastery" pillar. Heavier opinion pieces alongside lighter technical explorations.
_Novelty_: A blog too narrowly focused on AI consulting feels like marketing. Broad curiosity across technology feels like a real person — builds trust.

**[Site Architecture #3]**: Homepage Flow — Empowerment-Led
_Concept_: Hero leads with client outcome ("Help your team create more value, solve problems faster, use tools like AI to their full potential") → POV block (experienced guide philosophy) → Proof points (career credibility) → Recent blog posts → How I work → Simple CTA.
_Novelty_: Leading with "here's what I can do for your team" rather than "here's who I am." Visitor sees themselves in the message before evaluating the messenger.

**[Site Architecture #4]**: Tech Stack — Stay on Astro + AI-Assisted Design
_Concept_: Remain on Astro 4 with Markdown content collections. Invest in clean, professional design system (typography, spacing, color, layout) with AI assistance for CSS/SCSS. Simple stack, frictionless content workflow, free GitHub Pages deployment.
_Novelty_: Don't migrate your stack to fix a design problem. Practicing what you preach — using AI to amplify capabilities.

**[Site Architecture #5]**: Site Map — Lean Five Routes
_Concept_: Home (/), Blog (/blog), Blog posts (/blog/[slug]), About (/about), Contact (/contact). No services page (POV and blog do that work), no portfolio page (case narratives live in blog), no industry verticals.
_Novelty_: Restraint is the design choice. Five routes signals confidence — "I don't need to fill pages to prove my value."

**[Site Architecture #6]**: Contact — Low Friction, Direct Email
_Concept_: Simple "Send me an email" with mailto link + LinkedIn. No contact form, no lead capture. Brief line like "Want to talk about how AI can help your team? Drop me a note."
_Novelty_: Removing the form builds trust. A form says "sales funnel." A direct email says "you're talking to a person." Matches "experienced guide" positioning.

**[Content Idea #1]**: "What People Are Getting Wrong About AI"
_Concept_: Flagship article challenging the "AI = cost reduction" narrative. Argues the real opportunity is amplification — making teams, products, and solutions more valuable, not just cheaper. Draws on SI and manufacturing experience.
_Novelty_: Contrarian, backed by operational credibility. Gives people language for what they already felt but couldn't articulate.
